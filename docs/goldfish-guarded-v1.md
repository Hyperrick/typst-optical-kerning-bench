## Guarded V1 Rules

The active candidate is `guarded-profile-hybrid`.

It combines:

- metric kerning as the prior
- robust profile whitespace as the optical signal
- nearest contour distance as a guard
- aperture risk detection to avoid over-tightening open shapes
- a small safe compaction for uncritical spacing pairs

Important constants in this baseline:

```text
metric/optical disagreement pull: 0.80
nearest guard: target_gap_em * 0.08, clamped to 0.012..0.020em
aperture close-min: target_gap_em * 0.42, clamped to 0.040..0.120em
safe compaction: gap_mad_em * 0.25, clamped to 0.008..0.016em
global clamp: -0.16..0.16em
dead zone: 0.006em
```

whole instead of optimizing a single sample.
